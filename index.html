Settings

Riding

Touch lock: tap the padlock to lock; hold it for about a second to unlock. Swiping between pages and the Lap button still work while locked. 700x25c = 2105 mm, 700x28c = 2136, 700x32c = 2155.

Live tracking

Friends open the link to see your position, trail, speed, HR and distance, updated about every minute while a ride is running. Updates are encrypted; only people with the link can read them. Garmin LiveTrack on your watch works too.

Emergency

Hold the red SOS button for 2 seconds, even when the screen is locked. You then get one-tap Call and Text buttons with your location, and live followers are alerted automatically.
